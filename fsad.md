This Project enables Customer to shop online i.e place orders and search for the products under various categories This Project also enables admin to monitor the online sales and also to manage the products available to the customers.he is also enabled to view reports about the online sales happened for analysis.

1)Logins are made available separately for the admin and the Customer                                                                              2)Registered customers should be able to view the products(All details-Product Id,Name,Description,Price ,QOH) category wise(Electronics,Electrical,Furniture,Cosmetics,Toys & Books ) and product wise                                 3)Customers should be able to add the products to a shopping cart and also remove it from the cart                                                4) Customers should be able to order the goods  that are added to the cart and also delete the orders.                                                         5) Customers should be able to send feedback                                                                                                                                                                                      6) Admin should be able to login and update the product s under various categories like Electronics,Electrical,Furniture,Cosmetics,Toys & Books Details should include Product Id,Name,Description,Price ,QOH(add/delete/modify)                                                                                                                                        7)Admin should be able to view the sales happened over a week,Month,Qtr & Year-Productwise as well as Category wise sales.        (Sales Details are understood to be already updated using the previous module)                                                                                                                                                         8) Reports should be made available for the sales of the products and also regarding the Customer Feedback   9)Administrator should be able to analyse based on the sales the fst moving and slow moving products.
